About Marina Leaf

Where Quality Meets the Waterfront

Marina Leaf brings a more refined approach to the modern smoke shop — combining premium products, thoughtful presentation, and a relaxed harbor atmosphere in one distinctive retail experience.

Our selection brings together vapes, cigars, cigarettes, tobacco, smoking accessories, exotic snacks, cold drinks, and boat-day essentials. Every category is carefully presented to make the store easy to explore, whether you're stopping in for something familiar or discovering something new.

Located near the Marina Del Rey harbor, Marina Leaf is designed to be more than a quick stop. It’s a polished, convenient destination built around quality, variety, and the character of the surrounding marina community.
